This page is for you if your company's IT support put the NeoDesk app on
your computer. With it you can ask your support team for help, write to them
and tell them when something isn't working. You don't have to set anything
up.

## Open the app

The app has an icon in the notification area, at the bottom right of the
screen next to the clock. If you don't see it, click the small arrow there.

- Click the icon to open the app.
- Right-click it for a short menu: **Open NeoDesk**, **Get help**,
  **Messages** and **My tickets**.

When your support team wrote to you or answered a ticket, the icon gets a red
dot, and the menu shows how many are new, for example "Messages (1 new)".

The window shows your support team's name at the top and has three areas on
the left.

![The app with Get help open: a field for the code or link, and Messages and My tickets on the left](shot:receiver-home)

## Get help

Use this when someone from your support team is about to help you on the
phone or by mail.

1. Click **Get help**.
2. Type the code or paste the link they gave you into **Code or link**, and
   click **Start**.
3. The app shows who wants to help you and what they can do. If that is
   right, click **Allow**. If you don't expect this, click **Decline**.

Nobody sees your screen before you click **Allow**. No code? Click
**Write a message** and ask your support team.

## Messages

**Messages** is a chat with your support team. Type your message and press
Enter. To show them what you see, click **Add a picture of my screen**: the
app window hides for a moment, takes the picture and adds it to your message.

When your support team answers, a notification appears. Click it to open the
chat.

## My tickets

Use a ticket when something isn't working and it can wait until someone
looks at it.

1. Click **My tickets**, then **Something isn't working**.
2. Under **What's wrong?**, tell them what you were doing and what happened.
   Your own words are enough.
3. If it helps, click **Add a picture of my screen** or **Add files**.
4. Click **Send**.

![The form Something isn't working with a description, a picture of the screen and a file](shot:receiver-ticket-form)

Your name and details about this computer go with the ticket, so your support
team knows where to look.

The list under **My tickets** shows each ticket with where it stands:
**Received**, **Being worked on**, **Solved** or **Closed**. **New answer**
marks a ticket with something you haven't read. Click a ticket to read the
answers and to write back. When there is news, a notification "News about
your ticket" appears; click it to open the ticket.

## The bar at the top of the screen

While someone from your support team is connected, a bar sits at the top of
your screen:

![The bar at the top of the screen: Sam Keller is viewing and controlling your computer, with Chat, Stop and a button to show details](shot:client-bar)

- It says who is connected, for example "Sam Keller is viewing and
  controlling your computer". While they use the mouse or keyboard, it says
  "is using your computer". When two or more people are connected, it says how
  many.
- **Chat** opens a chat with them under the bar. **Files** shows up when files
  are sent between the two computers.
- The arrow on the right shows the details: what they are allowed to do,
  with a switch for each. Turn one off to take it back.
- You can move the bar: drag it by the dots on the left.

To end the session, click **Stop**. Everyone connected is disconnected at
once, and the bar goes away.
